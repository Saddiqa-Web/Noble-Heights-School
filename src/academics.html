Academic Excellence

Our Curriculum

At Noble Heights, we follow an integrated curriculum that blends international standards with local requirements. Our approach focuses on critical thinking, creativity, and character building.

  • Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math (STEM) focus
  • Language Arts and Multilingual Proficiency
  • Physical Education and Arts Integration

Learning Stages

Tailored education for every Noble Heghts

Primary School

Focusing on foundational literacy, numeracy, and social-emotional development.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Basic Literacy & Phonics
  • Number Sense & Logic
  • Arts & Creative Play

Middle School

Encouraging independent thinking and deeper subject exploration. Our middle school program bridges the gap between foundational learning and advanced reasoning, empowering students to question, analyze, and innovate in a collaborative environment.

Core Subjects:

  • Integrated Science & Math
  • History & Global Studies
  • Digital Literacy

Senior School

Preparing students for higher education and professional careers with specialized exam preparation.

Specializations:

  • Pre-Engineering & Medical
  • Computer Science
  • Career Counseling
